## Estimation workflow

For each candidate, classify current photos; review room condition; propose specific improvements and their low/high cost; review renovated comparable sales; estimate conservative/base/optimistic resale values. Save exact comp IDs and dated source facts. Do not use an unadjusted maximum sale as the resale target.

For a ten-month hold, solve break-even purchase price B as:

B = [resale × (1 − sale closing rate) − repairs − HOA × 10 − other fixed holding costs] / [1 + purchase closing rate + (annual interest rate + annual property tax rate) × 10/12]

Initial editable assumptions: sale costs 6.5% including commission, purchase costs 1%, interest 10% on full purchase price, property tax 1.1% of purchase price, known HOA monthly fees. Insurance excluded at user request. Renovation financing, utilities, contingency and permits must be listed explicitly if included. A target-profit offer also subtracts the desired profit from the numerator. Report an offer range driven by low resale/high repair and base scenarios; break-even alone provides no profit cushion.
